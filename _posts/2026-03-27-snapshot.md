Snapshot is an image-first English learning app.

You upload or capture a picture, the app extracts a core English word from the image, and then saves the result into study history, check-ins, and word books.

Snapshot 是一个“拍图学英语”的应用。

上传或拍摄图片后，应用会从图像里提取核心英文词汇，并把结果保存到学习历史、打卡和单词本里。
